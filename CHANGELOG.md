# Changelog

All notable user-facing changes to Buzzreel are documented in this file.

## 2.1.2 — 2026-08-25

### New features and improvements

- Regional movie and TV feeds now prioritize locally produced live-action titles,
  while continuing to include popular local animation and titles available from
  the user's selected streaming providers.
- Regional results now alternate movies and TV shows for a more balanced mix of
  content instead of reordering both categories solely by rating.

### Fixes

- Reduced duplicate titles when combining local and streamable regional results.
- Prevented animation-heavy catalogs from crowding locally produced live-action
  titles out of regional feeds.
